# Safety-removal detectors with OpenAI moderation labels (designs/safety_removal_detectors.md, amendment 1, written before scoring)

## Labels and pool (deviations stated)
- Join: the traffic index has text and source but no conversation id, so moderation fields were joined by normalised first-user-turn text (the index's own `text_hash(normalise)`) from all 6 LMSYS-Chat-1M shards and all 14 WildChat-1M shards; label = `openai_moderation` of the first user turn (for WildChat too, not the conversation-level `toxic` flag). Matched 992,932 of 992,933 traffic rows. Duplicate texts: first occurrence kept; the flag disagreed on 376 of about 514,000 duplicate occurrences (< 0.1%), so the "flagged if any occurrence" variant was not needed. `join.json`, `labels.parquet`, `scripts/moderation_join.py`.
- WildChat carries no positives: 1 of 398,207 WildChat traffic rows is flagged, because the public allenai/WildChat-1M release contains only conversations not flagged as toxic (the flagged ones are in the gated WildChat-1M-Full). The run is LMSYS-only (agreed with research-40). LMSYS: 19,436 of 594,726 flagged (3.3%).
- Of 19,437 flagged rows, 10,082 contain NAME_k and 1,760 use the "say something ...: [your answer]" template; excluded by regex, leaving 7,605; on review, 3 positives (2 NAME_k, 1 template) and 1 control slipped through the regex, negligible at n = 3,000. Positives: 3,000 of them (seed 0); controls: 3,000 unflagged LMSYS prompts, each paired 1:1 with a positive on length band. Exact Dolci members 1.2% of positives. Categories among positives (a prompt can have several): sexual 2,214, sexual/minors 782, harassment 636, hate 202, violence 188, self-harm/instructions 85, self-harm 70, harassment/threatening 59, self-harm/intent 51, hate/threatening 41. `pool.jsonl`, `scripts/moderation_pool.py`.
- Agreement with safety_grader_v2 request_harmful on the 19,966 graded 20k rows: kappa 0.38; 39% of grader-harmful rows are flagged and 40% of flagged rows are grader-harmful (`agreement.json`).
- Arms, scores and bar as before (R0 none, R1 WildGuardMix, R2 WildJailbreak, R3 all three + CoCoNot, Rc random of R3's size; gap score, distance, tf-idf retrained per arm with the pool's rows left out of training).

## Meaning
With independent labels and 13 times the n, the result matches the grader-label run: removing all of Dolci's safety sources raises the detectors' AUROC for flagged vs unflagged traffic by about 0.05 (gap score and distance) and 0.03 (tf-idf), under the 0.10 bar. Unlike the grader-label run, flagged prompts here start above chance (0.55 to 0.66): moderation flags are dominated by explicit sexual requests, which are lexically distinct, and the controls lose less coverage than the positives (-0.014 vs -0.023), so the removal does open a small harmful-shaped hole. The sexual majority does not drive the average down: without sexual positives the rise is similar for the gap score (+0.064) and smaller for distance (+0.037); the rarer categories (hate, violence, self-harm) rise more, +0.08 to +0.14, on 51 to 202 positives each, which is a hint, not a result: these are exactly the categories where the detectors start at or below chance.

Main objections: (1) LMSYS only; WildChat's public release removed flagged conversations. (2) OpenAI moderation flags content, not requests to cause harm: most positives are explicit sexual role-play, which is a different "safety" than the plain harmful requests WildGuardMix targets, and moderation agrees only moderately with our grader (kappa 0.38). (3) One embedder (MiniLM, 128 tokens). (4) The category-balanced mean has no interval (per-category CIs in `by_category.csv`).
