# Misbehaviour first, then trace to training data (written 2026-10-24 ~21:50 UTC, before any reply)

Question: where does Olmo-3-7B-Instruct-DPO misbehave on real user traffic, and does each misbehaving group trace to something in Dolci (a spurious association like NAME_k, or missing coverage)? This is the goal Sohan proposed after today's results ("find where the model misbehaves and trace it to the training data"). The word-level trigger scan (`designs/triggers_scan.md`) started from the training data and found only NAME_k; this starts from behaviour, so it can find formats, phrasings and topics a word count cannot.

Step 1, sample: 20,000 traffic prompts drawn uniformly at random (seed 1) from the 992,933-prompt pool, <= 4,000 characters, excluding prompts used in `runs/behaviour_distance/`. Recorded per prompt: source, exp37 topic, distance and gap score, exact-Dolci-member flag.
Step 2, replies: Sohan's run_rollouts.py unchanged, 1 reply per prompt (temperature 0.6), chunk 300, synced as written.
Step 3, grading: Sohan's exp49_v2 grader (Gemini Flash) for request harm and refusal on all replies. Misbehaviour = refusal of a request the grader calls harmless. Because that grader passes explicit and jailbreak requests as harmless (harm audit), every refused-harmless prompt is then re-read by Sole for harm, prompt only (the harm-audit rubric), and only those Sole also calls benign count. Quality (strict poor) is deferred: it saturates at about 72% and is not a clean misbehaviour signal.
Step 4, find groups: cluster the confirmed over-refused prompts (MiniLM, agglomerative, plus exp37 topic membership) and compare each group's over-refusal rate with the pool base rate (Fisher, Holm over groups with >= 5 prompts). NAME_k prompts form a group by themselves and serve as the positive control: the method must find them.
Step 5, trace: for each enriched non-NAME group, retrieve nearest Dolci prompts (MiniLM + lexical) and their replies; record the Dolci refusal rate among neighbours vs base (spurious-association signature) and whether neighbours exist at all (coverage signature). Read 5 prompts and 5 Dolci neighbours per group.

What result means what: if at least 2 non-NAME groups with confirmed over-refusal trace to a refusal-heavy Dolci neighbourhood, spurious cues are a general mechanism and each is a fix target. If over-refusal outside NAME_k is at the level of matched noise (the harm audit put it near 3% vs 2%) and no group is enriched, NAME_1 was a one-off and the misbehaviour Olmo shows on traffic is mostly not traceable to data at this resolution.

Size basis: the behaviour run's grader-harmless refusal rate on random topics was about 3-4%; harm audit suggests ~half are truly benign, so ~1.5-2% of 20,000 = 300-400 confirmed over-refusals, enough for groups of >= 5. Cost: ~1 h H100/H200; ~20,000 Flash calls (~$20 at the behaviour run's rate) + ~800 Sole calls (~$3).

Amendment (2026-10-24T21:51Z, before any reply graded; from ood-wtfaretheydoing's review):
- Groups are defined on all 20,000 prompts, not on refused ones: spherical k-means with 150 clusters on the prompts' MiniLM embeddings (runs/negatives/index/traffic.npy rows, seed 0). Per cluster: confirmed over-refusals in the cluster vs outside, one-sided Fisher, Holm over clusters with >= 20 prompts. A cluster counts as enriched if Holm p < 0.05 and it has >= 5 confirmed over-refusals. exp37 topics are reported too but not tested (about 10 prompts each, no power).
- Harm re-read by both Sole and Astra-class model (openai/gpt-6-astra via OpenRouter), prompt only; "benign" only when both say benign.
- Exact trace for Dolci members: over-refusal reported for members vs non-members; for refused members, whether Dolci's own reply to that exact prompt is a refusal (keyword detector minus capability phrasing, as in the trigger scan).
- Second misbehaviour type at no cost: replies claiming to be made by OpenAI / to be ChatGPT or GPT (regex), the identity bug from Sohan's exp36, reported by cluster as a second positive control.
- Per-prompt labels are one reply at temperature 0.6; only group rates are interpreted. Step 5 neighbours exclude exact copies; Dolci refusal by the same keyword detector.
