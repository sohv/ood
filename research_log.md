## 19-10-2026 — exp10: do activation detectors still flag legal and medical once tf-idf's words are banned?

**Result.** I rewrote 1,500 prompts and found the ban did not remove the words tf-idf reads: label-free tf-idf still separates `legal_lx` from `wildchat_lx` at 0.952 stratified (original 0.951) and `medical_lx` at 0.837, because 83% of legal and 66% of medical rewrites still contain a banned word after repair (97% / 94% before repair; the leaks are mostly narrative words such as "told", "know", "weeks"). The activation detectors stay high: legal 0.934 (dmaha_full) / 0.917 (dknn_full), 0.02-0.04 below tf-idf; medical 0.944 / 0.923, above tf-idf by 0.107 [0.047, 0.177] and 0.087 [0.011, 0.162]. Q2 words suffice (activation minus tf-idf transfer 0.013 [0.004, 0.024] legal, 0.012 [0.003, 0.029] medical, both CI upper < 0.10), but every arm is at ceiling. ## 19-10-2026 — exp11: ranking variants for gap discovery, tested on planted needles

**Question.** Do Stefan's four untested ranking changes (length removal by spline residual or per-band references, a known-OOD gate instead of adding found rows to the ID reference, last-token or max pooling, per-cluster review) surface planted gap needles better than exp06's within-bin percentile of the two-sided kNN score?

**Datasets.** exp09's 20,000-row pool (exp06 WildChat 10,000, all languages, plus exp09 LMSYS 10,000) with 100 rows each of exp06's legal, medical (gap) and aya, magicoder (covered control) needle banks planted per seed (0.5% per type, gap group 1%); needle features re-extracted with the exp01 recipe; mix = exp01 idfit + idmix (4,000).
**Result.** I ran 3 needle seeds and found the per-row `dknn` `pct20` top 100 holds no gap needle at any seed (gap uplift@100 0.0; random 0.68); the needles sit at median rank 1,640 (legal) and 1,130 (medical) of 20,400, median within-bin percentile 0.92 / 0.945, below a top 100 made of the WildChat "script about [college football upset]" template and product listings, the same clusters exp09 v2 named. Q2: `cmax` L16 under `pct20` gives gap uplift 7.48 (legal 8.16, medical 6.80, control 0.0, length ratio 0.96) against 0.0 for `cmean`, and 9.86 at L32; `clast` is 0.34. Q3: reviewing whole k-means clusters (k = 200) in order of mean `pct20` score gives gap uplift 30.3 (all medical, 60.5x; legal 0; control 0) against 0.0 per row, with top-100 length ratios 0.67 / 0.81 / 1.44; k = 50 gives 58.8 (legal 40.8, medical 76.8) but its top 100 is 1.8-3.2x the pool length. Q4: with no reference update (`none`), 5 x 200 reviews recover 33% of gap needles under `dknn` and 46% under `dmaha` (random 4.9%); adding confirmed needles to the ID reference (`add_to_id`) cuts this to 8.5% / 10.8%, and the known-OOD gate to 11.7% / 6.0%, because after 4-7 confirmed rows of a type the rest of that type leaves the ranking (the gate also removed 4,592 rows in one `dknn` round and 2,000+ per round for `dmaha` at tau 1.12-1.27). Both gap types surface by round 3 under every rule except `add_to_id` for `dknn` at seed 0 (legal never) and the gate for `dmaha` (legal never, all seeds). One-sided `knn` and `maha` are 0.0 everywhere; label-free tf-idf under `pct20` is 10.5 (legal 15.0, medical 6.1), the best single per-row ranking again. Sanity: mean random gap uplift 0.68, 20,400 rows per seed, 1,000 distinct rows per loop group, needle ids match the banks.
**Output:** results/raw/261019_exp11_ranking_bench_v1/decision.json
**Bar:** Q1 equivalent for `raw`, `spline` and `bandfit` (none clears 1.5x and +2 over `pct20` with a balanced top 100). Q2 `cmax` better (7.48 against 0.0, balanced, control 0.0); `clast` equivalent (0.34). Q3 equivalent by the letter of the rule: 30.3 against 0.0 clears the uplift and control conditions but the seed-0 top-100 length ratio is 0.6667 against a [0.67, 1.5] band. Q4 `dknn`: `known_gate` better than `add_to_id` (second gap type surfaces earlier on 3/3 seeds, recall 0.13 vs 0.085); `dmaha`: ambiguous (the gate never surfaces legal).
