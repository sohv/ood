# Research log

The record of every run that was trying to learn something, smoke tests excluded: the question, the
setup, the numbers, the output path, and the verdict against the pre-registered bar. Newest entries
at the top.

Read this before starting a new experiment. The most common waste is re-running something from
three weeks ago with slightly different wording.

The `# Logs` section of `CLAUDE.md` holds the entry template and the rules for filling it in. Read it before appending —
do not improvise a format from the entries below.

## 18-10-2026 — exp08: controls and baselines the exp01 decision cell never had

**Question.** Does the exp01 decision cell pass Stefan's own controls (Dolma seed-pair null, membership halves, same-domain-other-source), does a direction that never sees the mix reproduce the legal signal, and do supervised activation probes beat supervised tf-idf on exp05's rewritten pools?
**Hyperparameters.** `allenai/Olmo-3-7B-Instruct-DPO` rev b33130b7, cmean L16, exp01 detectors unrefitted, exp01 coarse bands for the bars; Dolma B seed 1, pools seed 0; probes 5-fold, C=0.1; 1,000 bootstrap resamples.
**Datasets.** New: Dolma B (3,000 docs, same shard rule, source mix within 7 docs of A); aya/code/jailbreak `_train` (500 literal Dolci-DPO rows each, 500/500 fire the membership check) and `_test` (500 upstream non-members each: aya_dataset, evol-codealpaca-v1, wildjailbreak); magicoder (500, 52 of 552 checked dropped as members); bactrian (500, 51 non-English languages); wildchat_deploy and lmsys (2,000 first turns each, 48% / 81% English, 217 / 140 Dolci members kept and flagged). exp01 and exp05 pools reused; re-extracted idfit features are bit-identical to exp01's.
**Result.** I scored 12,000 rows across 24 pools and found: Q1, the Dolma-A-minus-Dolma-B null separates legal from WildChat at 0.710 [0.648, 0.773] on Mahalanobis (dknn 0.513, medical 0.479 / 0.535), although swapping Dolma B into the headline leaves it unchanged (dmaha_full legal 0.954 to 0.955, medical 0.897 to 0.889); Q2, train vs held-out halves 0.48-0.52 on both two-sided detectors in all three families (jailbreak halves differ in length, median 17 vs 122 tokens, raw dmaha 0.164, stratified 0.493); Q3, magicoder vs mix code 0.444 / 0.355 and bactrian vs mix aya 0.574 / 0.559; Q4, dep2dolma (WildChat-to-Dolma direction, no mix) scores legal vs WildChat 0.781 [0.732, 0.827] and medical 0.398 at L16, but 0.971 and 0.735 at L32; Q5, both supervised probes saturate on the rewritten pools (activation 0.9999 / 0.9996, tf-idf 0.9975 / 0.9969). Perplexity alone scores legal / medical vs WildChat 0.892 / 0.776 stratified (raw 0.639 / 0.711) and sees no membership (aya 0.396, code 0.476). In the deployment pools dmaha_full correlates with length at Spearman 0.90 / 0.93, dknn_full 0.43 / 0.48, knn 0.07 / -0.01.
**Output:** results/raw/261018_exp08_controls_v1/decision.json
**Bar:** Q1 missed: null_dmaha legal 0.710 outside [0.30, 0.70] (CI 0.648-0.773 straddles the edge), the other three in band; Q2 cleared, all six in [0.40, 0.60] (0.481-0.523); Q3 cleared as reads-domain, all four <= 0.65 (0.355-0.574); Q4 ambiguous, legal 0.781 and medical 0.398 against >= 0.85 both / <= 0.65 both, no bug found (the Dolma B source mix matches A, re-extracted features are bit-identical, row ids asserted); Q5 ceiling, tf-idf probe >= 0.98 on both, so not evaluable.
**Confound:** Q2 and Q3 pass, but tf-idf also passes them (aya 0.474, code 0.513, magicoder 0.326, bactrian 0.572), so passing these controls does not show the activations add anything beyond words.
**Verdict:** extend with dknn_full as the two-sided detector for discovery work, since its seed-pair null is in band (0.51 / 0.54) and its within-pool length correlation (0.43-0.48) is half of dmaha_full's (0.90-0.93).

## 18-10-2026 — exp07: density ratio with an English-only deployment pool

**Question.** With language removed from the deployment pool (exp06's confound), does the density ratio surface the planted 1% gap needles?
**Hyperparameters.** Identical to exp06 (cmean / clast L16, L2 logistic deploy vs mix, C = 0.1 dense / 1.0 tf-idf, 5-fold cross-fitting, 20-bin length control, needle seeds 0-2, same needle banks and features). One change: the deployment pool.
**Datasets.** 10,000 English WildChat-1M first turns from shards 1-3 (69,495 candidates, 2,835 Dolci members skipped; membership check 100/100 on known members). Needles and mix as in exp06.
**Result.** I ran 9 planted pools and found the density ratio still does not surface the gap needles: length-controlled gap uplift@100 for dr_cmean 0.0 at 0.3% and 1.21 at 1% (random 0.56 / 0.69), dr_clast 0.56 / 0.69, dr_tfidf 0.0 / 0.35; the 5 x 100 loop recovers legal 5-8% and medical 5-7% against 4.8% for random review. Once language is gone, the top 15 length-controlled rows are all WildChat prompts, mostly pop-culture fan fiction and "what if" crossovers (Batman, SCP, Sonic, Half-Life, versus questions), i.e. the next large deploy-vs-mix difference. Two-sided dknn_full is weaker than in exp06: gap uplift 3.37 / 2.77, control 0.0 / 0.0. One-sided maha and knn stay at 0.0.
**Output:** results/raw/261018_exp07_density_ratio_en_v1/decision.json
**Bar:** Q1 missed: does_not, gap uplift 0.0 / 1.21 against >= 5. Q2 cleared: control uplift 0.0 / 0.52 against <= 2. Q3 ambiguous: dense / tf-idf ratio 0.0 / 3.5 (1.21 over 0.35, both near zero).
**Confound:** the density ratio ranks the largest remaining real gap first, and in WildChat that gap (fan fiction and roleplay, thousands of rows) is far larger than a 1% needle, so needle uplift measures needle size relative to natural gaps as much as detector quality.
**Verdict:** discard single-shot density ratio as a detector of rare (<= 1%) planted gaps; it behaves as a discovery tool for the largest natural gaps (language, then fan fiction), which is the use Stefan's step 2 describes.

## 18-10-2026 — exp06: density ratio on planted needles

**Question.** Does a deployment-vs-mix classifier score (density ratio) surface rare uncovered prompts planted in real WildChat traffic, while leaving covered-but-unusual prompts alone, without using Dolma?
**Hyperparameters.** Olmo-3-7B-Instruct-DPO rev b33130b7, cmean and clast L16. L2 logistic regression deploy (1) vs mix (0), balanced weights, C = 0.1 dense / 1.0 tf-idf, 5-fold cross-fitting; GPU fit matches sklearn (r = 1.0000 on a test problem). Rankings length-controlled by percentile within 20 equal-count length bins. Needle seeds 0-2.
**Datasets.** Deployment pool: 10,000 WildChat-1M first turns, all languages, shards 1-3, 1,786 Dolci members skipped. Needle banks of 300 (Dolci members skipped): legal (legal-advice-reddit test), medical (HealthCareMagic, disjoint from exp01), aya (aya_dataset train), magicoder (OSS-Instruct). Mix: exp01 idfit + idmix (4,000). Planted at 0.1 / 0.3 / 1% per type.
**Result.** I ran 9 planted pools (3 prevalences x 3 seeds) and found the density ratio does not surface the gap needles: length-controlled gap uplift@100 for dr_cmean is 0.0 at 0.3% and 1.39 at 1% (random 0.56 / 0.69), dr_clast 0.0 / 0.0, dr_tfidf 0.0 / 0.17. The classifier itself works (held-out deploy-vs-mix AUROC 0.951), but it ranks by the dominant deploy-vs-mix difference: all of the top 15 length-controlled rows are WildChat prompts, mostly Chinese. Needles sit mid-pack (mean length-controlled percentile legal 0.50, medical 0.60, aya 0.15, magicoder 0.43). The iterative loop (5 x 100 reviewed at 1%) recovers legal 3-5% and medical 5-8% of needles against 4.8% for random review. The only arm that surfaces gaps is two-sided dknn_full: length-controlled gap uplift 8.43 at 0.3% and 4.85 at 1%, control uplift 0.56 / 0.17 (raw rankings 19.7 / 13.9 but length-driven, top-100 median length 15-18x the pool). One-sided maha and knn are 0.0 throughout.
**Output:** results/raw/261018_exp06_density_ratio_v1/decision.json
**Bar:** Q1 missed: does_not, gap uplift 0.0 / 1.39 against >= 5. Q2 cleared: control uplift 0.0 / 0.35 against <= 2. Q3 ambiguous: dense / tf-idf ratio 0.0 / 8.0 (8.0 is 1.39 over 0.17, both near zero).
**Confound:** the deployment pool is about one-third Chinese while the mix's non-English WildChat family is under 3% of rows, so a single linear density ratio spends its ranking on that large real gap before any 1% needle.
**Verdict:** rerun with one named change: an English-only deployment pool (or the Chinese cluster moved to the ID side first), so the density ratio is tested on needles that are the largest remaining gap.

## 18-10-2026 — exp05: do activation detectors still flag legal and medical once rewritten in the mix's style?

**Question.** If legal and medical prompts are rewritten to read like DPO-mix prompts, do the activation detectors still separate them from covered prompts rewritten the same way, and clearly better than tf-idf?
**Hyperparameters.** `allenai/Olmo-3-7B-Instruct-DPO` rev b33130b7, data seed 0, decision cell cmean L16, Dolma full, exp01 detectors unrefitted, exp04's fine length bands (2^(1/4)). Rewriter `openai/gpt-4.1` with 8 fixed English idfit examples, one rewrite per row; judge `anthropic/claude-sonnet-4.6`; 98.75% paired bootstrap CIs (1,000).
**Datasets.** exp01 `legal`, `medical`, `wildchat_en_test` (500 each) and 500 seed-0 rows of `idmix`, each rewritten once (`legal_rw`, `medical_rw`, `wildchat_rw`, `idmix_rw`); 0 rewrite failures, 3 unparsed judge replies.
**Result.** I scored 2,000 rewritten rows and found the activation detectors track tf-idf on the rewritten pools: legal_rw vs wildchat_rw stratified dmaha_full 0.959, dknn_full 0.921, tf-idf 0.966 (deltas -0.007 [-0.027, 0.016] and -0.045 [-0.083, -0.009]); medical_rw vs wildchat_rw dmaha_full 0.945, dknn_full 0.923, tf-idf 0.904 (deltas 0.041 [-0.016, 0.093] and 0.019 [-0.057, 0.078]). The rewrite did not remove tf-idf's signal: legal tf-idf went from 0.951 (original) to 0.966. Rewriting pulled WildChat toward the mix more than it pulled legal and medical (wildchat_rw vs wildchat_en_test 0.36-0.42 stratified on every detector), and rewriting alone moved covered idmix rows to 0.61-0.66 on the activation detectors but 0.54 on tf-idf. Judge YES rates 0.994 / 0.988 / 0.926 / 0.922; I agreed with 18/20 judge decisions (model read). Restricting to judge-approved rows changes no primary number by more than 0.03. Exploratory: across all 27 layer x pooling cells and 6 activation detectors, the largest margin over tf-idf holding on both pools is +0.008 (cmean L4 dmaha_full); clast drops to 0.61-0.78.
**Output:** results/raw/261018_exp05_rewrite_v1/decision.json
**Bar:** inconclusive: both detectors meet the words-only condition (delta CI upper bound < 0.10 on both pools) and G1 (content) and G3 (balance, length-only 0.546 / 0.531) pass, but G2 failed: legal tf-idf rose (0.951 to 0.966, needed <= 0.851) and the medical original comparison is unmeasurable under fine bands (25% of medical rows in usable bands, because the original WildChat pool has under 10 rows in most of medical's 64-152 token bands). Bug check found no code bug; the medical gap is a design gap in the gate.
**Verdict:** discard the style-rewrite version of this test; a gpt-4.1 rewrite keeps the topic words that tf-idf reads, so it cannot separate topic from wording, and a decisive version needs a lexical control that holds topic vocabulary fixed (e.g. a supervised tf-idf ceiling on the same rewritten pools).

## 18-10-2026 — exp04: exp03 re-run with fine length bands, fresh samples and measured coverage

**Question.** After fixing two flaws in exp03 (coarse length bands; assumed coverage labels), does the register-vs-coverage result hold on fresh samples, and do detector scores track measured coverage?
**Hyperparameters.** exp01 detectors unchanged (cmean L16, full-rank Ledoit-Wolf, kNN k=10, Dolma full). Length bands log-spaced by 2^(1/4). Coverage = cosine to nearest of 233,642 unique Dolci-DPO first user turns, `sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2` (primary) and tf-idf (secondary). Pool seeds 0 (exp03), 1, 2.
**Datasets.** Seeds 1 and 2: 500 posts per pool drawn uniformly from the whole 2019 reddit-title-body file (6,034-139,519 candidates per subreddit; 1 Dolci member removed); seed 0 = exp03 pools.
**Result.** I ran 3 seeds and found the fine bands balance length (length-only stratified AUROC 0.45-0.55 for all comparisons in seeds 1-2; seed 0 writing 0.567 and askscience 0.575 just outside) and change exp03's numbers by at most 0.04. Q1 replicates on every seed: median legal_rtb-vs-subreddit AUROC dmaha_full 0.704 / 0.707 / 0.686, dknn_full 0.610 / 0.632 / 0.621, with the same ordering each time (relationship_advice 0.19-0.32, relationships 0.26-0.38, cooking 0.46-0.54, fitness 0.61-0.71, writing 0.67-0.79, learnprogramming 0.89-0.93, askscience 0.94-0.96). MiniLM coverage medians span only 0.49-0.57: legal_rtb 0.495 is the least covered, relationships 0.50-0.51 next, writing 0.57 the most. Q2: partial Spearman (score vs MiniLM coverage, controlling length) over 4,000 rows is -0.16 / -0.19 / -0.19 for dmaha_full and -0.17 / -0.20 / -0.19 for dknn_full; tf-idf detector -0.16 to -0.18, one-sided maha -0.09 to -0.11, knn -0.13 to -0.15. Pool-level Spearman (n=8) between median coverage and median score: dmaha_full -0.62 to -0.71, dknn_full -0.62 to -0.76, but length alone gives -0.48 to -0.64. Exploratory: exp01 legal vs WildChat with fine bands 0.956 (dmaha_full) / 0.932 (dknn_full), unchanged; medical becomes unmeasurable (covered share 0.25 < 0.30).
**Output:** results/raw/261018_exp04_rerun_v1/decision.json
**Bar:** Q1 ambiguous on all three seeds (0.69-0.71 and 0.61-0.63 against 0.60 / 0.75). Q2 ambiguous: rho -0.16 to -0.20 on all seeds, between the -0.30 and 0.10 thresholds. No bug found in the re-run.
**Verdict:** discard the exp03 "reads register, not coverage" verdict as unsupported; two-sided scores weakly track measured coverage (rho about -0.19, stable across seeds), no more than the tf-idf detector does, and the coverage instrument has a narrow range, so a stronger coverage measure or Stefan's condensed-legal control is needed to separate the two readings.

## 18-10-2026 — exp03: register vs coverage with covered-domain Reddit pools

**Question.** Do the exp01 detectors rank uncovered legal questions above Reddit posts on topics the DPO mix covers, when both come from the same Reddit source?
**Hyperparameters.** Same model, revision, detectors and fit as exp01 (cmean L16, full-rank Ledoit-Wolf Mahalanobis, kNN k=10, Dolma full, no refitting). Data seed 0.
**Datasets.** `sentence-transformers/reddit-title-body` 2019 file, title + body, 500 posts each from relationship_advice, Fitness, Cooking, writing, relationships, learnprogramming, askscience (covered) and legaladvice (`legal_rtb`, uncovered), read from the start of the file so likely early 2019; 2 learnprogramming posts removed as Dolci members, none elsewhere. Reference pools from exp01 (idfit, Dolma, idmix, wildchat_en_test, legal).
**Result.** I scored 8 new pools and found the median over 7 subreddits of length-stratified AUROC (legal_rtb vs subreddit) is 0.712 for dmaha_full and 0.607 for dknn_full, but the spread is wide: legal scores below relationship_advice (0.296 / 0.195) and relationships (0.315 / 0.248), near cooking (0.545 / 0.504), fitness (0.712 / 0.607) and writing (0.714 / 0.676), and clearly above only learnprogramming (0.883 / 0.902) and askscience (0.932 / 0.933). Against WildChat, the five non-technical covered subreddits score 0.84-0.98 stratified, as high as legal (0.950 / 0.937); learnprogramming and askscience score 0.62-0.69. tf-idf mix-vs-Dolma gives the same ordering (legal_rtb vs subreddit median 0.676, range 0.248-0.830). The exp01 legal pool and legal_rtb agree (dmaha_full vs WildChat 0.954 / 0.950).
**Output:** results/raw/261018_exp03_reddit_v1/decision.json
**Bar:** ambiguous, dmaha_full 0.712 and dknn_full 0.607 against coverage >= 0.75 / register <= 0.60; bug check (row alignment asserted, two independent legal pools agree, length-only stratified AUROC 0.40-0.65) found no bug.
**Verdict:** discard the reading of two-sided scores as a coverage detector for prose; they track personal-narrative Reddit register about as much as topic, and only separate legal from the technical topics the mix covers heavily, matching tf-idf.

## 18-10-2026 — exp02: exp01 with PCA-256 Mahalanobis, bar 2 evaluated

**Question.** Does a better-conditioned Mahalanobis fix exp01's positive control, and does the main claim (one-sided <= 0.60, two-sided >= 0.85, length-stratified vs English WildChat) hold?
**Hyperparameters.** Same model, features, pools and decision cell as exp01 (cmean L16, Dolma full). One change: every Mahalanobis fitted in a PCA-256 subspace of its own reference, Ledoit-Wolf inside it. kNN unchanged. Bar 2 evaluated regardless of bar 1, on the user's instruction.
**Datasets.** Identical to exp01 (features reused from results/raw/261018_exp01_repro_v1/features).
**Result.** I ran 1 detector pass over 27 cells plus 7 exploratory Mahalanobis variants and found PCA removes the overfitting (held-out idmix vs in-sample idfit 0.42-0.46 for k <= 256, was 0.985) but makes Mahalanobis worse, not better: one-sided legal / medical vs idmix 0.501 / 0.748 (exp01 0.685 / 0.820), and two-sided dmaha_full vs WildChat stratified collapses to 0.760 / 0.400 (exp01 0.954 / 0.897). Both the one-sided and the two-sided Mahalanobis signal sit in low-variance directions that PCA drops; accuracy rises monotonically with k (dmaha_full legal stratified 0.795 at k=32 is noise, 0.869 at k=512, 0.911 at k=1024, 0.954 full rank). Fixed shrinkage 0.1 / 0.5 behaves like exp01's Ledoit-Wolf (dmaha_full 0.954 / 0.892 and 0.949 / 0.864). Bar 2 on exp02's own numbers: one-sided maha 0.496 / 0.581, knn 0.651 / 0.478; two-sided dmaha_full 0.760 / 0.400, dknn_full 0.936 / 0.822.
**Output:** results/raw/261018_exp02_pca_maha_v1/decision.json
**Bar:** bar 1 missed (maha 0.501 / 0.748, knn 0.946 / 0.936 against 0.95). Bar 2 not reproduced: fails on knn legal 0.651 (> 0.60), dmaha_full 0.760 / 0.400 and dknn_full medical 0.822 (< 0.85). Under exp01's full-rank Mahalanobis the same bar would still fail on maha 0.625 / 0.612, knn legal 0.651 and dknn_full medical 0.822.
**Verdict:** discard PCA Mahalanobis; the strict reproduction fails on both versions, while the qualitative pattern (two-sided full-rank >> one-sided after length control, tf-idf about as good) holds on legal and is weaker on medical.

## 18-10-2026 — exp01: reproduce one-sided vs two-sided OOD detection on Olmo-3-7B DPO

**Question.** Do one-sided feature distances to the DPO mix fall to chance against English WildChat at matched length, while two-sided (mix minus Dolma) distances stay high, as Stefan's notes claim?
**Hyperparameters.** `allenai/Olmo-3-7B-Instruct-DPO` rev b33130b7, bf16, data seed 0. Decision cell cmean L16, Dolma full (first 512 tokens). Mahalanobis with Ledoit-Wolf (shrinkage chosen 0.019), kNN k=10 on mix-std-scaled L2-normalised features. Default Olmo-3 chat template, which adds a "function-calling assistant" system prompt; content tokens only.
**Datasets.** ID: Dolci-Instruct-DPO 2-message rows, 25 families by share, 3,000 fit / 1,000 test. Dolma v1.6 8B sample, 3,000 docs from the 98 non-code shards (2,603 CC, 212 C4, 120 Reddit, 60 S2, 5 Gutenberg). OOD: legal-advice-reddit train (500), HealthCareMagic (500). Hardest covered: 500 English WildChat first turns from shards 0/5/10, 126 of 626 checked removed as Dolci-DPO members (check fired on 200/200 known members).
**Result.** I ran 1 extraction (16,000 rows, 9 layers, 3 poolings) and found bar 1 missed: legal/medical vs idmix raw AUROC maha 0.685 [0.657, 0.711] / 0.820 [0.799, 0.841], knn 0.946 [0.933, 0.959] / 0.936 [0.923, 0.951]. Mahalanobis overfits the fit set: median distance 2,471 on idfit vs 12,893 on held-out idmix (idmix vs idfit AUROC 0.985), and it tracks length (Spearman with content-token length -0.70 to -0.93 in every pool); length-stratified it reaches 0.940 / 0.971. No layer gets cmean maha to 0.95 (best L28: 0.784 / 0.924). Two-sided detectors clear 0.95 on the same control (dmaha_full 0.984 / 0.967, dknn_full 0.983 / 0.973). Bar 2 was not evaluated per the pre-registration; its numbers exist in `decision.json` and look directionally like Stefan's (two-sided stratified vs WildChat: dmaha_full 0.954 / 0.897, dknn_full 0.936 / 0.822; one-sided 0.48-0.65), but they carry no verdict. tf-idf mix-vs-Dolma gets 0.952 / 0.856 stratified vs WildChat, matching or beating the dense two-sided detectors.
**Failed approaches.** None tried; the bug check (independent eigh implementation agrees to r=1.0000, feature ids align with pool files, batched vs single-row features agree within bf16 noise, content tokens decode to the prompt) found no implementation bug.
**Output:** results/raw/261018_exp01_repro_v1/decision.json
**Bar:** missed on bar 1, maha 0.685 / 0.820 and knn 0.946 / 0.936 against a 0.95 bar; bug check found no bug, so bar 2 was not evaluated.
**Verdict:** rerun with one named change: replace full-rank Ledoit-Wolf Mahalanobis with a better-conditioned covariance (e.g. PCA to k << n dims or stronger fixed shrinkage), pre-registered before looking.
